# Timer Rack privacy policy

Timer Rack does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The four timers (each one's set time, and when it ends or ended) are saved on your device
in the app's own storage, so they survive the app closing. They're included in your own
device backup like any app's data. The app never syncs, uploads or sends them anywhere,
and it makes no network requests.

If you turn on off-screen alarms, the app schedules each alarm with iOS on your phone. The
notification says only the glass's colour and its set time.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.
