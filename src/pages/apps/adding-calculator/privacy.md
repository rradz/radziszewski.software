# Adding Calculator privacy policy

Adding Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The numbers you type are saved on your device only, in the app's own storage, so a sum
survives the app closing. They are not synced, uploaded, or sent anywhere. The app makes
no network requests.

The copy key writes to your clipboard only when you press it, and only the number shown.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.
