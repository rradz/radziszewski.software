# Bare Page support

Bare Page is one page of plain text. It opens on your page, ready to read; a line is
whatever sits between two Returns, and fine rules separate one line from the next.

**Write:** tap **+** to start a new line at the end and type. Tap any line to change it:
the cursor goes where you tapped. Everything is saved as you type; there is no save button.

**Copy line:** copies the line the cursor is on, or what you've selected. With the
keyboard down, it copies the whole page.

**Paste:** while you're writing, puts what you copied right where the cursor is, as plain
text on the same line, handy for dropping an amount or a code into a note. With the
keyboard down, it adds what you copied as a new line at the end of the page.

**Share:** sends the line the cursor is on (or what you've selected) to another app, a
message or a file. With the keyboard down, it sends the whole page.

**Delete line:** removes the line the cursor is on: tap the line, then Delete.

**Undo:** steps back through your last changes, one at a time: typing, deleting,
pasting. It works for 15 seconds after your last change; then, or when you leave the
app, the history is gone for good. Bare Page keeps no hidden history.

**Hide keyboard:** puts the keyboard away so you can read the whole page. In its place
is **+**. If you start a new line and leave it empty, it goes away again.

On an iPhone Duo opened out, the lines take turns between two columns, left and right,
so you read across and then down, and you write in place.

The page follows your phone's light or dark setting. It stays on your phone.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
