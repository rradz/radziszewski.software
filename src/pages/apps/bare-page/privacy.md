# Bare Page privacy policy

Bare Page does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What you write is saved in one file on your device, in the app's own storage, so the page
survives the app closing. It is included in your own device backup like any app's data,
and is never synced, uploaded, or sent anywhere by the app. The app makes no network
requests.

Undo keeps your last few changes in memory only, for 15 seconds after your last change,
and forgets them when you leave the app.

Copy and Paste use your clipboard only when you tap them. Share sends text only to the
app or person you choose in the share sheet.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.
