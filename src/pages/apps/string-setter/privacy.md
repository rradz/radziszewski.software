# String Setter privacy policy

String Setter does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app uses the microphone while it is open to hear the pitch of your guitar. Sound is
analysed on the phone as it arrives and discarded; nothing is recorded, saved, or sent
anywhere. The app makes no network requests.

One preference is saved on your device only: the tuning you last chose.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.
