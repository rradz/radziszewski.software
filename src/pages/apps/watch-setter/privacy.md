# Watch Setter Privacy Policy

Watch Setter does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

The app makes one request to Apple's public time server (time.apple.com) each time it is
opened, to check the phone's clock. That request carries no identifier and nothing is
stored from the reply beyond the clock offset, kept in memory while the app is open.

Two preferences are saved on your device only: the time zone chosen for the GMT hand and
the hemisphere chosen for the moon disc. They never leave the phone.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.
