# Watch Setter Support

Watch Setter is a single screen for setting a mechanical watch.

**Set in** counts down to the next full minute. Pull the crown, set the hands to the time
shown under **Set to**, and push the crown in when the dial reads **Now** and the phone
buzzes.

**Date, Day, Month, Year** are the values for the watch's calendar windows.

**GMT** shows the hour for a 24-hour GMT hand in the zone of your choice. Tap it to pick
another zone.

**Moon** shows how many clicks to advance a moon-phase disc from the new moon or the full
moon, whichever is nearer. Tap to switch between the northern and southern view.

**Date quickset** warns when the date mechanism of most watches is engaged (9 PM – 3 AM),
when quicksetting the date can damage the movement.

The screen stays on while the app is open.
